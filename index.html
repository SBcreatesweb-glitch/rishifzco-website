SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainable Wood Recovery Solutions

Rishi International FZCO is proudly recognised as an EHS Approved Third-Party Wood Scrap Collection Partner within Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA).

We assist companies operating within JAFZA by providing prompt collection of unwanted wooden pallets, plywood, timber and other non-hazardous wood materials, helping businesses maintain clean, safe and efficient industrial facilities.

Recovered materials are responsibly sorted for refurbishment, reuse or recycling, supporting a circular approach to industrial wood management while reducing unnecessary waste.
